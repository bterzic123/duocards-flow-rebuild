What this is

We went through DuoCards the way a new learner does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at DuoCards: installed it, walked the onboarding from the first question to the paywall and past its close button, read your App Store listing and your FAQ, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

Your flow is already strong — it asks good questions and turns them into a path from one level to the next. What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — the mammoth and its speech bubbles, the outlined progress bar, your level bars, the interest tiles, the A0–C2 card and your blue pill buttons. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us — we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
